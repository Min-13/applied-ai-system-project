Mochi's Care Notes — Indoor Cat

Mochi (Domestic Shorthair, age 5) has been on a special diet since January 2026.

Diet: Mochi should eat Hills Science Diet Urinary Care, half a cup twice daily at 8 AM and 6 PM. Do not free-feed as Mochi tends to overeat. Avoid dry food from other brands.

Water intake: Mochi prefers running water. A pet water fountain is strongly recommended to encourage hydration and prevent urinary issues.

Grooming: Brush Mochi twice weekly to reduce hairballs. Monthly nail trim required. Mochi tolerates grooming well with treats.

Behavior: Mochi is anxious around strangers and loud noises. Allow 15–20 minutes to adjust when new people visit. Feliway diffuser helps reduce stress.

Medical history: Mochi had a urinary blockage in December 2025. Monitor litter box usage daily — straining, crying, or no output for 12+ hours is an emergency.

Medications: No current medications. Previous treatment: prazosin for urinary relaxation (completed February 2026).
